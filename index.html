About YUVA SETU

From labour-market signals to actionable skill intelligence

Instead of functioning only as a student skill-comparison portal, YUVA SETU creates a continuous ecosystem connecting industry demand, district skill trends, qualifications, curriculum, training and employment outcomes.

Industry Demand

Job roles, required skills, proficiency levels and employer demand create the foundation of the platform.

District Intelligence

Skill demand is analysed according to location instead of depending only on national averages.

Curriculum Alignment

Industry-required skills are mapped against courses, curriculum, trainers and equipment.

Outcome Feedback

Placement and employer feedback continuously improve skill-demand and training recommendations.
